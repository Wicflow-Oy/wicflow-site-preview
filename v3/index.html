What we believe

The work that slows a company down isn’t in any software. It’s in inboxes, spreadsheets and phone calls. We find that work and build for it, one block at a time.

Our approach

Built in blocks, highest return first.

We don’t start from a product. Every block is chosen for what it returns to your company.

How we startFree · 2–4 weeks

First we build something for you. At our cost.

We’d rather show you than pitch. So we start with a short pilot: one useful piece of work, built for your company, before you pay anything.

  1. Day 1

    We pick one thing together

    In a 30-minute call we find one thing that costs you time or deals, and agree how we’ll know it works.

  2. 2–4 weeks

    We build it on your real work

    With your customers, your quotes and the systems you already use.

  3. Then

    You decide what’s next

    If it’s worth it, we build the next block at a price agreed in writing. If not, you keep what we made and owe us nothing.

What a pilot can be

A few examples. We choose the one that matters most for you.

  • No forgotten quotes

    Reminders and drafts, so every open quote gets its follow-up on time.

  • Faster replies to enquiries

    Draft answers to new enquiries, ready for your team to check and send.

  • Companies ready to buy

    Companies in your area that need what you sell right now, each with the reason.

  • A first email campaign

    Personal emails to hand-picked potential customers, with the replies sorted for you.

Agreed in writing. One pilot per company, with what’s included set down before we start, and larger systems only once we both know they’re worth it.

Questions

What people usually ask first.

Still have a question? Write to us.
info@wicflow.com

How is this different from other AI offers?

Most offers are a ready-made tool that your company has to adapt to. We work the other way round: we learn how you work, estimate what automating each process would return, and build the one that pays back most first. Nothing gets built that you don’t need.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing. We agree in writing what the pilot includes, usually one clearly limited piece of work over two to four weeks, and we do it at our cost.

What do we keep if we don’t continue?

What we made for you in the pilot: for example the lists, the email texts, the meetings booked and any automation built into your own tools. Things that run on our platform, such as Company Brain, keep running only under a monthly agreement with us.

What does it cost after the pilot?

Each next block is priced in writing before we start, and running what you keep has one monthly price. For reference, a website costs €1,480 to build plus €240 a year for hosting, and Company Brain starts at €1,900 a month. Prices exclude VAT (25.5%).

Do we have to switch systems?

No. What we build connects to what you already use, such as Pipedrive, HubSpot, Outlook, Gmail, Netvisor and Procountor.

Does AI send anything without our approval?

You approve the target group and the texts before a campaign starts. Replies to customers go out only once someone on your team has approved them. Company Brain never sends anything on its own.

Where is our data?

Stored in Finland: on servers in Helsinki, backups included. The AI runs in the EU, and under our agreements with the AI providers nothing you send is stored and your data is never used to train AI models.
