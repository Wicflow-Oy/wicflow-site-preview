Is this a CRM?

No. Company Brain sits on top of your CRM and your other systems. Deals stay in Pipedrive or HubSpot, and Company Brain shows what needs doing with them.

Who sees what?

Each person sees only what their role should see. Salespeople see their own deals, management sees the whole picture. You set the permissions yourself.

Can we ask it questions from Claude or ChatGPT?

Yes. Company Brain can be connected to Claude so you can ask it from there, with the same permissions as in the system.
