Common questions

What people usually ask first.

What does it cost?

It depends on the tier, your company's revenue and your users. Lite starts at €490 a month plus €49 per full user, Standard at €1,990 plus €129 and Max at €3,900 plus €179. The platform fee is higher for companies with more than €5M in revenue, and the price per user is the same for everyone. Light users cost €9 to €19. Setup is a one-time fee from €990, paid when everything is running. Prices are for a yearly agreement (month to month costs 25% more) and exclude VAT.

What is a light user?

Someone who mainly uses the mobile app: clocking in and out, their own tasks and requests, such as field or warehouse staff. Full users use everything in the tier.

What is the AI budget?

Every tier includes AI use up to €500, €1,500 or €5,000 a month, with our model that runs in the EU. Most companies use far less. You see the usage in Company Brain, and we tell you before you reach the budget.

Do we have to switch systems?

No. The systems connect to what you already use, such as Pipedrive, HubSpot, Outlook, Gmail, Netvisor and Procountor.

Does AI send anything without our approval?

You approve the target group and the texts before a campaign starts. Replies to customers go out only once someone on your team has approved them. Company Brain never sends anything on its own.

Where is our data?

In the EU. Company Brain and the AI it uses run in EU data centres, and your data is never used to train AI models. Every connection shows what it sees and what it never does, and you can switch it off yourself.

How fast can we get started?

Sales Radar can be running within a few days. Outreach needs about two weeks to warm up the inboxes. For Company Brain it depends on how many systems need connecting, and the timeline is in the quote.

Does it work in Finnish and Swedish?

Yes. The lists, the emails and Company Brain work in Finnish and Swedish, and in English when needed.

What if we want to stop?

After the minimum term you can end the agreement. All your data can be exported.
