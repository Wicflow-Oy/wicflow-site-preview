Users

Full users and light users.

Full user

Uses everything in the tier: Ask, quotes, sales, reports and the plugins you have switched on.

Light user

Mainly the mobile app: clocking in and out, their own tasks and requests. Field and warehouse staff are usually light users.

AI use

A monthly AI budget is included.

Included

Common questions

About tiers and users.

Can we change tier later?

Yes. You can move up whenever you want, and down at the end of the agreement period. Your data and settings stay.

What counts as a user?

Everyone with their own login. You add and remove users yourself, and the price follows the number of users each month.

What if we need a plugin that doesn't exist?

We build it for Wicflow OS, so it's updated together with everything else. You get a fixed price in the quote.

What if we want to stop?

The minimum term is in the quote. After it you can end the agreement, and all your data can be exported.

Which tier fits you?

A 30-minute demo with Felix or Robin. We show Wicflow OS with examples from your industry and tell you which tier fits.
