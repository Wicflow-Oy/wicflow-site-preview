Terms

Yearly or monthly, your choice.

What's the difference between yearly and monthly?

A yearly agreement costs 20% less, the price is locked for 12 months, and you can try another product free for a month. Month to month costs more, and you can stop after the first three months. Both are paid monthly.

Is setup included?

Yes. We connect your systems, set everything up and train your team. Anything built specifically for you, such as new plugins or integrations, is quoted separately at a fixed price.

What decides the final price?

The size of your company, how many people use it and what needs connecting. You get the price in writing in the quote before you decide.

Is AI use included?

Yes. Company Brain includes AI use up to a monthly budget, with our model that runs in the EU. Most companies use far less. Frontier models are available in Wicflow OS Complete, billed by use with a limit you set.

Can we add products later?

Yes, whenever you want. Everything shares the same data, so a new product is up and running quickly.

What if we want to stop?

All your data can be exported when you leave.
