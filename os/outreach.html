Common questions

About the rules and your email.

Are B2B sales emails allowed in Finland?

Yes, when the email relates to the recipient's work and they can easily opt out. Every email includes an easy way to unsubscribe. We've written a guide to the rules (in Finnish).

Does this affect our normal email?

No. The emails go from separate domains we set up for you, so your normal email and your reputation aren't affected.

Do you promise a number of meetings?

No. The number of meetings depends on your offer. We track volume and reply rate every week and improve the emails together with you.
